Regulated Operations

Platforms for work where source evidence, professional judgment, corrective action, approval, and audit history must remain connected.

Working platform · Seeking controlled-pilot partners

Maven

Maven is a regulated-operations platform designed to turn source records into traceable investigations, findings, corrective work, approvals, and regulatory outputs.

Maven Healthcare is the first working industry implementation, built for post-acute and long-term-care operations. The same evidence, review, corrective-action, and approval model is designed to adapt to other highly regulated industries.

  • Evidence-grounded investigations and reporting
  • Plans of Correction and QAPI workflows
  • Professional review and deterministic document boundaries
Visit Maven → Discuss a pilot →

Working prototype · Controlled-pilot candidate

FairFight

FairFight is a consumer evidence and case-intelligence platform that preserves source documents, derives traceable evidence, and supports reviewed deterministic checks.

  • Matter, source, evidence, and chronology workflows
  • Candidate Findings kept distinct from human Decisions
  • Constrained, citation-validated synthesis
Discuss a controlled pilot →

Maven and FairFight are separate products built with the same discipline: preserve the source, keep machine findings as candidates, leave decision authority with people, and retain durable history.

Computation & Infrastructure

Engineering systems for deterministic computation, infrastructure coordination, authority, evidence relationships, and durable operational history.

Active product development

Helix Compute

Deterministic selective execution for data pipelines and stateful workloads. Helix constrains computation to affected work while equivalence checks compare its result with full recomputation within validated workloads.

Its computation core has been engineered extensively and validated under controlled workloads; the public interface, API, and delivery model remain in active design.
